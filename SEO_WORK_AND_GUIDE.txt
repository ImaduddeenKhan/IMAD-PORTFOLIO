PART 1 — WHAT I CHANGED IN THE CODE
================================================================

1) A real SEO module: lib/seo.js
   - For every one of your 22 industry pages I wrote:
     * A primary keyword (the main phrase that page is trying to rank for).
       Example for healthcare: "AI automation for hospitals and clinics".
     * About 8–12 long-tail keywords per domain.
       Long-tail = longer, more specific phrases like
       "how to implement AI in hospitals" or
       "AI receptionist for clinics India" — these are easier to rank for
       than short generic words like "AI" or "hospitals".
     * 4 frequently-asked-questions per domain, written in the exact way
       a doctor / restaurant owner / lawyer / etc. would actually type
       into Google.
   - This file also generates:
     * The full <title> tag (this is the blue link in Google results).
     * The <meta description> (the gray text under the blue link).
     * Open Graph + Twitter card tags (controls how the link looks when
       shared on WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Twitter).
     * A canonical URL (tells Google "this is the official version of
       this page" — prevents duplicate-content penalties).
     * Robots directives (allow indexing, allow following links).

2) Structured data (JSON-LD) for every domain page
   - Component: components/JsonLd.jsx
   - For each industry page I emit THREE schemas Google reads:
     a) Service schema  → tells Google "this page is a service offering"
        with a name, description, area served (India), and provider (you).
     b) BreadcrumbList   → shows breadcrumbs in search results
        (Home > Industry name) which improves click-through rate.
     c) FAQPage schema   → makes your FAQs eligible to appear directly
        inside Google results as expandable questions. This is huge for
        click-through because your listing takes up more vertical space.

3) Visible FAQ section on every industry page
   - File: components/portfolio/DomainPage.jsx
   - Google's rule: FAQ schema is only valid if the same FAQs are
     visibly present on the page. So I added a real, clickable FAQ
     accordion at the bottom of every domain page that mirrors the
     same questions as the JSON-LD.
   - This also gives the page more high-quality, keyword-rich text,
     which directly helps ranking.

4) Per-page metadata wired up
   - All 22 files at app/<slug>/page.jsx now use:
       export const metadata = buildDomainMetadata(domain);
     instead of the old generic title. So each page gets its own
     unique, keyword-targeted title + description.

5) sitemap.xml (auto-generated)
   - File: app/sitemap.js
   - Lists every important URL of your site (homepage, /projects,
     all 22 industry pages, the heavyhaul case study).
   - Google uses sitemaps to discover and re-crawl pages faster.
   - Live at:  https://YOUR-DOMAIN.com/sitemap.xml

6) robots.txt (auto-generated)
   - File: app/robots.js
   - Tells crawlers: index public pages, do NOT index /admin, /dashboard,
     /api, /auth.
   - Live at:  https://YOUR-DOMAIN.com/robots.txt
   - It also points Google to your sitemap.

7) Page-quality fixes that indirectly help SEO
   - Money calculations now scaled to a clearly-stated medium-business
     profile on every page (annual turnover, team size, locations).
     This means each page has unique, useful content — not the same
     generic numbers — which Google rewards.
   - "Live Case Study" duplicate buttons removed → cleaner crawl, less
     duplicate-link signal pointing to the same heavyhaul page.
   - Internal links from the homepage (Projects, AI for Business cards,
     Explore All Projects) now open in a new tab → keeps users on the
     main portfolio longer, which improves "session duration" signals.
   - Building section overflow fix → page no longer has a layout bug
     that hurts mobile UX scores (Core Web Vitals).
   - Hire Me + Download CV CTAs → clearer conversion intent, which
     indirectly boosts engagement metrics Google watches.

PART 2 — WHAT YOU NEED TO DO FROM YOUR SIDE
================================================================

The code is now SEO-ready. But ranking is 30% on-page (done) and
70% off-page + ongoing signals (your job, mostly free, just takes
consistency). Do these in order:

[ ] STEP 1 — Set your real domain
    - Make sure NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL is set to your live URL
      (e.g. https://imadkhan.dev) in your hosting provider's
      environment variables (Vercel / Render / Railway).
    - Without this, sitemap and canonical URLs will point to
      localhost and Google will ignore them.

[ ] STEP 2 — Verify the site in Google Search Console
    1. Go to https://search.google.com/search-console
    2. Click "Add property" → enter your domain.
    3. Verify ownership (easiest: DNS TXT record, or upload an HTML
       file to /public/, or paste a meta tag — GSC will guide you).
    4. Once verified, go to "Sitemaps" in the left menu.
    5. Submit:  https://YOUR-DOMAIN.com/sitemap.xml
    6. Click "Request indexing" for your homepage and your top
       3–5 industry pages (the ones you most want to rank).
    - This is THE single most important step. Without GSC, Google
      will eventually find you, but with GSC it happens in days
      instead of weeks.

[ ] STEP 3 — Set up Google Analytics 4 (GA4)
    1. Go to https://analytics.google.com → create a property.
    2. Get your "Measurement ID" (looks like G-XXXXXXX).
    3. Add it to your site (let me know and I will wire it in,
       or use a tag manager).
    - Why: it tells YOU which pages people are landing on, which
      keywords they searched, and where they drop off. You can
      then double-down on what works.

[ ] STEP 4 — Set up Bing Webmaster Tools (5 minutes, free traffic)
    - https://www.bing.com/webmasters
    - Submit the same sitemap. Bing also powers DuckDuckGo and
      ChatGPT search results.

[ ] STEP 5 — Create one social profile per industry page link
    Backlinks (other sites linking to yours) are the #1 ranking
    factor outside of content. Easy free backlinks:
    - LinkedIn profile → put your portfolio URL in "About" + "Featured".
    - GitHub profile README → link to portfolio + each domain page.
    - Medium / Dev.to → write 1 short post per industry, link back
      to that industry page on your portfolio.
    - Indie Hackers, Hashnode → same idea.
    - Quora / Reddit → answer real questions like "how to use AI in
      hospitals" and naturally link to your healthcare page when
      relevant. Do not spam.
    - Product Hunt → if you launch HeavyHaul or any product, link
      back to portfolio.

[ ] STEP 6 — Get listed in directories
    - https://www.crunchbase.com/  (create personal profile)
    - https://clutch.co/ and https://www.goodfirms.co/ — list yourself
      as an AI consultant.
    - https://www.upwork.com / https://www.fiverr.com profiles → link
      back to portfolio.
    - Local: list yourself on JustDial / Sulekha as "AI consultant"
      with portfolio link.

[ ] STEP 7 — Write 1 blog post per month for each top industry

    - Target ONE long-tail keyword from lib/seo.js.
      Example: "How to implement AI in a 50-bed hospital in India:

    - Be at least 1200 words, with real numbers and screenshots.

[ ] STEP 9 — Ask 3–5 past clients for a testimonial

PART 3 — HOW DOES GOOGLE ACTUALLY RANK PAGES? (PLAIN ENGLISH)
================================================================

Imagine Google as a librarian for the internet. When somebody
searches "how to implement AI in hospitals", the librarian has
to pick the 10 best pages in the world to show on page 1.

The librarian decides using roughly these signals (in order of
importance):

1) RELEVANCE — Does the page actually talk about that exact thing?
   - Your title, headings, body text, FAQs, and URL all need to
     contain the keyword and related words.
   - This is what lib/seo.js + the visible FAQ section give you.

2) AUTHORITY (backlinks) — Do other respected websites link to it?
   - Each backlink is like a vote. A link from a big site
     (Forbes, a university) is worth thousands of small links.
   - This is why Step 5–6 in Part 2 matter so much.

3) E-E-A-T — Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trust
   - Google's quality raters check: does the author exist, are
     they a real expert, do they have real experience?
   - Your portfolio already wins here — real name, real face,
     real shipped products, case studies, testimonials. Most
     "AI consultant" pages on the internet are anonymous content
     farms. You are not. That is a big advantage.

4) USER EXPERIENCE — When people click your page, do they stay?
   - Fast loading, mobile friendly, no broken layout = good.
   - This is why Core Web Vitals + the Building overflow fix
     matter.
   - If users land on your page and immediately hit Back (called
     "pogo-sticking"), Google demotes you. If they stay 2+ minutes
     and click around, Google promotes you.

5) FRESHNESS — Has this page been updated recently?
   - Re-publish or tweak each industry page every 3–4 months
     (add a new stat, a new FAQ, a new case study line).
   - That is why a regular blog (Step 7) is so powerful.

6) STRUCTURED DATA — Does the page tell Google what it is in code?
   - This is the JSON-LD I added (Service / FAQ / Breadcrumb).
   - It does not directly raise rank, but it makes your search
     listing visually bigger (FAQ accordions, breadcrumbs), which
     gets you more clicks → which over time DOES raise rank.

7) TECHNICAL SEO — Can Google's crawler even read the page?
   - Sitemap.xml ✔, robots.txt ✔, canonical URLs ✔, mobile-friendly
     ✔, HTTPS ✔ — all done.

PUTTING IT TOGETHER
-------------------
Right after deploy, you will start showing up for very-long-tail
queries (5+ word searches) within 1–2 weeks.
Within 1–2 months, with Steps 1–4 done, your industry pages should
be on pages 2–4 of Google for their main keyword.
Within 4–6 months of consistent backlinks (Steps 5–7), top pages
should hit page 1 for their long-tail keyword
(e.g. "AI automation for clinics India").
Top spot for short generic keywords like "AI for hospitals" takes
12+ months and is honestly not worth chasing — long-tail brings
better-quality leads anyway, because the searcher already knows
what they want.

That's it. The hard part (the on-page SEO) is done. The rest is
a habit: one backlink a week, one blog post a month, check Search
Console once a week. Do that for 6 months and you will be hard to
ignore.
