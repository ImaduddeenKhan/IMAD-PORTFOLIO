INTRO VIDEO SCRIPT — IMAD KHAN
=================================
Length: roughly 60 to 90 seconds when spoken at a normal pace.
Style notes for you while recording:
- Talk like you are explaining yourself to one person sitting across the table.
- Slow down on the lines that matter. Pause for half a second before "And honestly..." and before the closing line.
- It is okay to look away from the camera for a moment when you are thinking. That actually feels more real.
- If you stumble on a word, do not start over — just keep going. Small stumbles make it feel human.
- No need for fancy editing. One take, eye contact, decent light from a window, mic close to you. That is enough.
